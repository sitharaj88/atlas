## Relational (SQL)

- Tables, rows, columns. Strong schema.
- ACID transactions. Joins.
- Postgres, SQLite, MySQL.

Best for: most apps, especially those with relationships, integrity, and analytics.

## Document (NoSQL)

- Flexible nested documents (JSON).
- Often eventually-consistent.
- MongoDB, DynamoDB, Firestore.

Best for: rapid evolution where schema is uncertain, single-document workflows, massive horizontal scale.

## The default in 2026

**Postgres.** It's a great relational DB AND has excellent JSON support (`jsonb`), full-text search, vector indexes (pgvector), and listen/notify. You get document flexibility WITHIN a relational engine.

Pick MongoDB only when:
- Scale demands it.
- The data shape is genuinely document-shaped (heavy nesting, varying schemas).
- Your team already knows it deeply.
