## Tools

- **Playwright's `toHaveScreenshot`** — built-in, free.
- **Chromatic** — Storybook + visual diffs in the cloud.
- **Percy** — older, cloud diffs.
- **Lost Pixel** — open-source, GitHub-native.

## What to capture

- Stable components in many states (Storybook + visual diffs).
- Critical pages at common breakpoints.

## Avoid

- Time-stamps, "last updated" labels.
- Non-deterministic data.
- Animations (disable them in tests).

## A workflow

1. PR opens.
2. CI captures screenshots.
3. Compares against main's baselines.
4. Diffs over a tolerance fail the build, with screenshots in the report.
5. Reviewer accepts or rejects the diff in the UI.
