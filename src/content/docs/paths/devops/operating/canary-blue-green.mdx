## Canary

Deploy the new version to a small slice of traffic. Watch metrics. If healthy, ramp up. If not, roll back — only X% of users were affected.

```text
Old: ████████████████████ 100%
   ↓
New: █                    5%   ← canary
Old: ███████████████████  95%

(metrics good?) → ramp to 25%, 50%, 100%.
```

Implementation:
- **Load balancer weighted routing** (most platforms).
- **Service mesh** (Istio) for fine-grained.
- **Feature flag** at the request level for application-layer canary.

## Blue / green

Two complete environments. Switch DNS / LB from blue to green when green is ready. Roll back instantly by switching back.

Pro: instant rollback. Con: 2× infra cost during cutover.

## Pick

- **Canary** — gradual, ideal for risk reduction on every deploy.
- **Blue/green** — when you need atomic switchover, like for DB migrations or breaking changes.
