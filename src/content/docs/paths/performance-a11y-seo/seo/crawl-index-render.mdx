## The three phases

1. **Crawling** — Googlebot discovers URLs (links, sitemap, manual submission) and fetches them.
2. **Rendering** — for SPAs, Google runs JavaScript (in a headless Chromium) to see the final DOM.
3. **Indexing** — Google decides whether the page is worth keeping and what it's about.

## What this means for you

- **Server-rendered HTML** is always indexed promptly. SPA rendering is best-effort and can be days behind.
- **Hydration is optional from SEO\'s perspective.** What matters is the HTML in the response.
- **Bad redirects, slow servers, blocked robots.txt** kill indexing.

## Quick wins

- Ensure key pages have unique `<title>` and `<meta name="description">`.
- Submit a `sitemap.xml`.
- Verify in Google Search Console.
- Check `robots.txt` doesn't accidentally block /.
