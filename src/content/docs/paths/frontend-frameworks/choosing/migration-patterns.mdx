## Strangler fig

Build new features in the new framework, behind a feature flag. Migrate old features one at a time as you touch them. The old framework "strangles" away over months/years.

Pros: low risk. Cons: you maintain two stacks during migration.

## Micro-frontends

Different teams ship independent apps mounted in slots in a shell. Each can use its preferred framework.

Pros: team independence. Cons: bundle size, design-system fragmentation, navigation glue.

## Islands

The old page stays. Drop in islands of the new framework where you need them. Astro pioneered this; the pattern works for any "modern bit in a legacy page" scenario.

Pros: progressive. Cons: islands need careful state-passing across boundaries.

## What rarely works

- **Big-bang rewrite.** "We'll have it ready in 6 months" → 18 months → buggy → frustrated team.
- **"Let's keep both forever."** Dual maintenance is real. Pick a target.
